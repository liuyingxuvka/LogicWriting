# Compose a traveler-facing guide

The trip model supplies candidates, route options, traveler fit, weather boundaries, risks and reachable fallbacks. The travel composition supplies the sole guide structure: body sections, operational appendix, source/recheck placement, local texture and risk/fallback bindings. Set its `trip_plan` path and use ids that resolve in that model.

Keep the body connected prose about choices a traveler can make. Put transport, time, booking, walking-load and recheck details in the declared operational appendix where appropriate. Preserve concrete risk triggers and named reachable fallbacks. Do not turn a candidate list into an unexplained checklist or upgrade an illustrative plan into current availability.

Review the current ArtifactMap spans through the existing route dimensions: guide kind, traveler fit, unit responsibility, handoffs, narrative body, operational appendix, risk/fallback, source recheck and local texture. These replace the duplicate itinerary-side day interfaces, scene plan, experience promises and reader projection. Weather chronology and evidence remain domain checks in `trip_context.weather_evidence_summary`.

Keep internal workflow labels out of final prose. Use the exact current artifact, current route review and independent reader judgment for closure. A saved world failure, a fit gap or stale source checks cannot be repaired by wording the guide more confidently.
