# Computed long-form closure

Use the project mesh as input to `longform_closure_check.py`. It loads each
current domain surface and computes `surface_checks`, issues and a decision.
It never accepts a prewritten decision, required-surfaces pass checklist,
child-report status, or model-alignment claim as evidence.

The composition plan is the only unit tree. Novel/story ledgers retain arcs,
threads, promises, continuity, repairs and progress. Interfaces and reverse
outlines use `planned_unit_id`. Reverse outlines record observed events, reader
state, promise/arc movement and drift from actual prose. Empty ledgers,
unresolved drift, missing evidence paths and unknown units fail their native
checks. A planned or not-run WorldGuard adapter never becomes a pass.

Source requirements name requirements, optional `planned_unit_ids`, and local
`evidence_ref` paths. They do not self-report satisfaction. The source checker
resolves these paths; quoted RouteReview and reader judgment assess realization.

Final prose uses the same eight-step reader chain as every other route.
ArtifactMap reads exact bytes; SharedWriting binds required units and content
through unique actual quotations; ReaderAudit checks deterministic constraints;
RouteReview inspects all eight normal dimensions and the long-form explanation,
variation and register dimensions. ReaderJudgment preserves actual-text reverse
outline, scores, defects, repairs and provenance. `derive_closure.py` combines
these current results with current long-form surface checks.

Return to the earliest failed source surface. Literary beauty, originality and
unobserved reader responses remain judgment boundaries, never deterministic
claims. Keep scoped deferrals visible at chapter, volume, book and series levels.
