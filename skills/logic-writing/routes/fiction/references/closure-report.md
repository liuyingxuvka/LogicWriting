# Reporting computed closure

Report the current `derive_closure.py` result and its native issue codes. Do not
author a second pass checklist, copied fingerprints, child-report status map or
closure input that merely says its inputs passed. Long-form surface results are
computed directly from project-mesh source files, then consumed by the same
reader-chain closure owner used by the other routes.

Keep evidence boundaries explicit: source/canon support, story continuity,
WorldGuard consistency, actual manuscript realization, and literary judgment
are different claims. A specialist's missing, not-run, partial, GAP, FAIL or
boundary-exceeded result never becomes a pass through a polished summary.
Scope deferrals to chapter, volume, book or series. Keep material open promises,
blocking continuity drift, stale evidence, repairs and human-review items visible.

Report what changed, the current source surface that supports it, the remaining
boundary and the next useful action. A plan establishes intended story movement;
actual quoted spans, reverse outline and current native checks establish the
limited claim about its realization. Attractive prose alone does not close a
missing model, and a complete model alone does not prove the manuscript works.

If closure does not pass, return to the earliest failed source surface. Preserve
native field meanings and original statuses. Stop a revision that fixes none of
the identified defects and return the artifact with its audit note. Beauty,
originality and unobserved reader response remain explicit judgment boundaries.
