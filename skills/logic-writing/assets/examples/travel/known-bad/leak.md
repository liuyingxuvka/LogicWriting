# A quiet day around Old Market

Start at Old Market at nine, before the delivery carts fill its narrow lane. Walk from the north gate to the covered arcade and pause at the bakery window; the courtyard beyond it is a good place to sit before continuing to the riverside. Allow an hour for the market and another for the short walk beside the water. This is a gentle morning for someone who wants a few places to remember rather than a long checklist. SourceGuard

Return to the arcade for lunch. If steady rain has made the riverside slippery, stay under the arcade and use the small reading room beside the bakery instead; it is reachable directly from the market without the exposed riverside path. Finish there by three and return through the north gate. These are illustrative places and times, so verify opening hours and access before applying the route to a real destination.
